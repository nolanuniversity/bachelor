Programme Philosophy

Business Fundamentals. Emerging Technology. Real Execution.

"Learn the business. Master the technology. Build something real. Operate globally."

The Bachelor of Global Business & Technology is designed to develop a new generation of business professionals who understand both business fundamentals and emerging technologies, and who can apply that knowledge to real-world problems in a global environment.

The programme combines business, economics, finance, entrepreneurship, technology, artificial intelligence, data analytics, innovation, strategy, leadership and global markets into a single, integrated, applied degree.

Not a compressed traditional degree delivered online. A structured, intensive and highly practical online learning experience built around real capability.

Year 3 Capstone

Nolan Global Capstone — Three Pathways

Choose one: Build a Venture, Develop a Technology-Enabled Business Solution, or Complete a Consulting Project. The capstone results in a substantial, professionally presented digital portfolio or practical business output.

Year 3 Capstone

Three Pathways. One Substantial Outcome.

Every student leaves with a professionally presented digital portfolio or practical business output — not just a transcript.

PATHWAY 01

Build a Venture

Develop and launch a new business — from concept and validation through to business model, financials, go-to-market, and a live or pilot-stage venture.

PATHWAY 02

Develop a Technology-Enabled Business Solution

Solve a significant business problem using technology, AI or data — delivering a working solution, prototype or applied system with real business value.

PATHWAY 03

Complete a Consulting Project

Deliver a strategic project for an organisation — applying analytical, strategic and technology-enabled methods to a real, sponsored business challenge.
